Investigating freshwater biodiversity, ecological processes, and ecosystem functions under global change.

About Our Research

Our research team is based in the Department of Biology at the University of Patras and is dedicated to advancing the understanding and conservation of freshwater ecosystems. We have extensive experience in monitoring freshwater biodiversity and assessing the ecological quality of surface waters, with particular emphasis on the requirements of the EU Water Framework Directive (WFD 2000/60/EC). We investigate global and climate change impacts on freshwater ecosystems, integrating eco-hydrology to study how hydromorphology shapes ecological processes. In addition, our research highlights the critical role of aquatic macrophytes in driving ecosystem metabolism and supporting essential ecological functions.

Research Activities

Biodiversity Monitoring & Conservation

Monitoring and assessing the diversity, distribution, and conservation status of freshwater plants, while developing effective conservation strategies to protect species, habitats, and freshwater biodiversity.

Climate Change & Ecosystem Functions

Experimental and observational studies on how climate change affects aquatic communities, primary production, and ecosystem metabolism.

Eco-hydrology & Hydromorphology

Understanding the role of hydromorphological dynamics in shaping biodiversity and driving ecological processes in river systems.

Water Framework Directive (WFD)

Ecological quality assessments of inland waters in compliance with European environmental policies and conservation goals.
